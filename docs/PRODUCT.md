# Product

**One line:** quick, beautiful games you play with friends inside Messages, the way
GamePigeon proved people want, rebuilt properly for today.

## Who and why
iPhone users who already text friends and like low-stakes competition. GamePigeon
(4.0★, ~234K ratings, last new game in 2020) proves the format; its UI, content cadence
and ads leave room for a better product (GAMEPIGEON_BENCHMARK.md).

## Strategy (brief §1)
1. **Functional parity:** someone who knows GamePigeon understands us instantly.
2. **Modern rebuild:** original identity, animation, haptics, sound, result screens.
3. **Differentiation:** Rivalry Sets, rematch records, random game, collections, practice.

## Principles
- Low friction beats features. No configuration screen before a match by default.
- Messages is the play surface; the app is practice, identity, cosmetics, stats.
- No pay-to-win, ever. Cosmetics never touch rules (enforced: `GameRules` has no cosmetic input).
- No ads inside a Messages turn.
- Never claim delivery or read state we cannot observe.

## Success test (brief §56)
Can two friends who know GamePigeon open this, understand it, finish a game, enjoy it more,
and want a rematch, without instructions? Measured in M7 with real pairs.
